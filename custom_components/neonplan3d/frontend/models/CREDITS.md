# Tesla Model 3

[Tesla Model 3 by David_Holiday](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/tesla-model-3-123c10f376ec4f18b93c73afc382808b) is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The self-contained GLB is distributed unchanged from [SceneView](https://sceneview.github.io/models/platforms/tesla_model_3.glb). NeonPlan scales the model to the vehicle dimensions and renders its body in graphite. The source does not specify a model year.

## Tesla Cybertruck

[Tesla Cybertruck 2025 by Nieve5677](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/tesla-cybertruck-2025-0fe4980c8cbc441382bfb7d4cf9f092e) is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Based on the [Tesla Studio conversion](https://github.com/aditano/tesla-studio/tree/main/public/models/cybertruck-import). NeonPlan removes three detached export artifacts, centres the body, rotates it to face +Z and assigns vertex colours to the existing geometry. The visible mesh uses the 2.0316 m body width, 5.6829 m length (rounded to 5.683 m in the pack) and 1.7938 m medium suspension height in the [owner's manual](https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/cybertruck/en_us/GUID-12A976DD-EB60-431B-AFF1-5A37E95006DB.html).
